# 14 — Formula Registry (v0.2-draft)

Status: DRAFT (added in v0.2, AUD-010). This is the **single authority for equations**: every equation of the constitution has exactly one
stable ID here; other documents cite the ID (display equations are followed by **[F###]**). Symbols: [02](02-symbol-registry.md).
Dimensions: every row's `dim` expression is evaluated by `tools/doccheck/check_constitution.py` under the 03 `dimtable` ("n/a: …" marks
set-, logic- or law-valued formulas with no numeric dimension). Assumption IDs: [04](04-assumption-and-decision-registry.md).

Status vocabulary. Proof status: DEFINITION (true by definition), PROVED, PROOF REQUIRES ADDITIONAL ASSUMPTIONS, DISPROVED, NOT YET
PROVEN, UNDEFINED. Counterexample status: "none found" (searched in 08/09 red team) or the counterexample reference. Research status:
SETTLED (no open research) or the open question.
